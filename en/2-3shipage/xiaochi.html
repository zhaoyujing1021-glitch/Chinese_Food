Iconic Regional Street Foods (Xiaochi)

Discover the vibrant street culinary tapestry of China through cherished local snacks.

Beijing Douzhi (Fermented Mung Bean Drink): A grayish drink made from fermented mung bean residue. Its pungent sour aroma challenges newcomers, yet paired with crisp fried dough rings (jiaoquan), it is beloved by native Beijingers.

Stinky Tofu (Chou Doufu): Fermented tofu cubes deep-fried until blistered and crunchy, bursting with flavorful chili garlic brine. Renowned for its sharp scent and irresistibly savory umami interior.

Chengdu San Da Pao (Three Cannonballs): Steamed sticky rice dumplings hurled dynamically against metal gongs with three loud 'bangs', rolled in toasted soybean flour and sweet brown sugar syrup.

Liuzhou Luosifen (River Snail Rice Noodles): Rice noodles in a spicy river-snail broth, topped with sour fermented bamboo shoots, fried tofu skin, and peanuts. Intensely aromatic and globally famous.

Cantonese Braised Beef Offal (Ngau Zap): Simmered beef brisket, tripe, and daikon radish in rich bean paste broth, served piping hot on paper skewers.
