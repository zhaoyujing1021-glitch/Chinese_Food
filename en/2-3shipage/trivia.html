Dumplings: Main Staple vs. Side Dish

In Japan: A Savory Side Dish. Pan-fried gyoza with thin skins are the standard, commonly ordered as a side accompaniment to a bowl of white rice and soup—a classic 'carb on carb' pairing.

In China: The Primary Staple. Boiled dumplings (jiaozi) reign supreme as a standalone festive main dish with thick hearty skins and generous fillings. Eating dumplings with rice is virtually unheard of and often amuses locals.
